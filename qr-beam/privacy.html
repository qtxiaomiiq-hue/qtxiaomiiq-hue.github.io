QR Beam privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

QR Beam ("the app") sends files from one device to another by showing them as a sequence of QR codes that the receiving device scans with its camera. The Android app shows ads, which keeps it free. This page explains what happens to information when you use it.

The short version

  • Files you send or receive never leave your devices. They travel only as QR codes shown on one screen and read by the other device's camera. They are never uploaded to any server.
  • No accounts or sign-in, no in-app purchases.
  • We, the developer, don't run any servers and don't collect, store, share or sell your personal information.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

Camera

The app asks for camera permission only to scan QR codes when you choose to receive a file. Camera images are processed on your device in real time and are never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can refuse or revoke camera permission at any time; sending files still works without it.

Files

  • Sending: when you pick a file (or share one to the app), the app reads it only to turn it into QR codes on your screen. It does not keep a copy.
  • Receiving: a received file is checked for integrity and saved to your device's Downloads/QR Beam folder. The app shows a list of files it has received, read from that folder.

Ads from Google AdMob

The app shows a banner ad on some screens and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after a file has been received. Ads are never shown while a transfer is in progress. To show these ads, Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC) may collect and process:

  • The device's advertising ID, and device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • An approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped, and diagnostic data

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the About screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Permissions

Camera (to scan QR codes) and internet/network state (to load ads only). File transfers themselves never use the internet.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • Received files: delete them from Downloads/QR Beam with any file manager.
  • App data: Settings → Apps → QR Beam → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or use myadcenter.google.com.
