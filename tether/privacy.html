Tether: One-Button Swing privacy policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Tether: One-Button Swing ("the game") is a single-player game that works offline. The Android app shows ads, which keeps it free. This page explains what happens to information when you play.

The short version

  • No accounts or sign-in, no chat, no in-app purchases.
  • We, the developer, don't run any servers and don't collect, store, share or sell your personal information.
  • The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

What stays on your device

Your progress (best scores, trial times and stars, lifetime totals, unlocked skins) and your settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Ads from Google AdMob

The game shows two kinds of ad:

  • An occasional full-screen ad between runs, never during gameplay. New players see none for their first few runs.
  • An optional "watch ad to continue" video after a run ends. You never have to watch it.

To show these ads, Google AdMob (a service of Google LLC) may collect and process:

  • The device's advertising ID, and device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • An approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ad interactions, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped, and diagnostic data

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised ads are shown, and you can choose non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Data processed by Google is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The web version of the game contains no ads and makes no network requests.

Permissions

The app uses internet access (to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings). It requests no runtime permissions: no location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Erase all progress
  • Android: Settings → Apps → Tether → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app
  • Advertising data held by Google: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or use myadcenter.google.com

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and the revised version published at this address.
